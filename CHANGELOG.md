# Changelog

**[README](README.md)** · [Report an issue](../../issues/new)

Notable changes by version. Earlier releases are listed under [tags](../../tags).

---

## 2.11.0

**Shows from the venues and bands themselves.**

- Most shows now come from venues' and bands' websites and calendars, and each one links back to them. A show's
  page says who listed it, like **From Harbor Cove Cafe**.
- When TOPA has a show for a venue and start time, that's the one listed, even if the Ventura Rocks calendar has a
  different act then.
- The legend on band and venue pages reads **From bands & venues**, and About explains where shows come from.
- New venue: the Ojai Playhouse.

---

## 2.10.0

**Suggest an edit, and a way to reach TOPA.**

- Band and venue pages have **+ Suggest an edit**. It opens About at a new **Contact** section with TOPA's email, so
  anyone can send a fix or a question without a GitHub account.
- The links at the bottom of band and venue pages are shorter (**+ Add a show**, **+ Update info**, **+ Suggest an
  edit**) and fit on one row on phones, the same as in the car. Each one is easier to tap.

---

## 2.9.1

**New icons for Bands and Venues.**

- Bands is a guitar pick now, and Venues a theater marquee, on phones and in the car. The mic means karaoke, and the
  map pin still marks distance, your area and directions.

## 2.9.0

**Karaoke mode, and an About for bands and venues.**

- Settings has a new **Karaoke mode**. Turn it on and Home gets **Karaoke this week**: a strip of the next seven days
  with a dot on every karaoke night. Tap a day for that night's spots, or See all for every karaoke listing. Tonight
  leaves karaoke out so nothing shows twice. It works the same on phones and in the car.
- Karaoke is a genre now. Karaoke nights get a **karaoke** chip on the days they happen, search always offers it, and
  common misspellings like "karoke" and "kareoke" still find it.
- Weekly karaoke at The Shores, Feros Ferio, C Note Bar & Lounge and KJ's Neighborhood Bar, plus Feros Ferio's Sunday
  singer/songwriter open mic.
- 23 bands now have an About section, written from their own sites and pages, with their genres and links. Neon Blonde,
  Frank Barajas, Danny D and Ken Devoe got band pages.
- Venue pages have an About section too, and a Facebook link. The 18 busiest venues have one so far. The venue form has
  new **About the venue** and **Facebook** boxes, so venues can fix or add their own.
- A show can carry a short note on its page, like who hosts it.
- Home labels each show Today or Tonight by its own start time, and a fave that plays today no longer shows up twice.

## 2.8.0

**Home behind the TOPA button, and band bios.**

- Tap the TOPA circle for Home: your faves' next shows (each band once, with its day and time) and tonight's lineup.
  Shows at a faved venue are tagged ★ Fave venue, and faved venues take at most two spots so your bands always fit.
  See all opens Faves or Shows with a ‹ Home button.
- When you're on Home the TOPA circle glows. In the car it moved to the middle of the sidebar, with "Updated" back at
  the bottom.
- TOPA still opens on Shows. Settings can switch it to Home.
- The ⓘ on Home has About and new Settings: your area (Ventura County), your own greeting for Home (up to 24
  characters), and where TOPA opens. Ventura County left the top bar.
- Bands can add a short bio with the band form's new **About the band** box. It shows as an About section on the band
  page and its show pages, with its genres and hometown. Josephoenix is the first.
- A band's own genres now show first on its band page, show pages and rows, and its shows come up under those genres in
  filters and search. Ventura Rocks' tags are kept after them.
- The band's name on a show page opens its band page.
- Get the app only shows where it's one simple step: one-tap install, or iPhone's Share › Add to Home Screen. The
  README has the steps for every browser.
- The search box on wide screens mentions genres too.

## 2.7.0

**Tell us when a show is cancelled, and a new tab bar.**

- Rained out? Every show page has a **Cancelled or changed?** row. Tap it, pick what changed (it's cancelled, a new
  time, a new venue, or something else), and a short form opens with the show already filled in.
- Once a cancellation is approved, the show stays listed but is clearly marked: a **Cancelled** tag, the time crossed
  out, and the reason with a link to the post when there is one. Play all and the play buttons skip it, and the day's
  count says how many are cancelled. "It's back on" takes the mark off.
- A new date or time works the same way: type it in the form's **New date** or **New time** box, and once it's
  approved the show moves. It gets a **New time** tag in lists and "New time · was 7pm" on its page.
- Reports match the listed time, so when a band plays two sets at one venue on the same day, only the reported one
  changes.
- New venues and other fixes come in the same way and get fixed by hand for now.
- On phones, TOPA sits in a raised circle in the middle of the tab bar, between Bands and Venues, and still opens About.
- The tab you're on is coral, with a short line above it on phones and beside it in the car.
- Lists fade out just above the tab bar and stop clear of the circle, and the mini player moves up to make room.
- On phones, About slides up from the bottom with a grab bar, and a swipe down closes it (so does the "What changed?"
  sheet). With Reduce Motion on, it opens and closes without sliding.

## 2.6.0

**Venue photos behind the page header, and a better home for the fave star.**

- Venue pages have no picture tile anymore. The venue's photo fills the top of the page, from the top bar down past
  Play lineup, and fades into the list of shows. The buttons sit on frosted glass so they stay readable on any photo.
- A venue without a photo yet gets its own colors there instead, every venue page looks the same.
- Show pages put the venue's photo (or its colors) behind the show, with the band's round picture beside its name.
- Without the picture tile, shows start higher on the page. Even a small phone like the iPhone SE now shows the first
  shows without scrolling.
- The fave star found better spots. On venue pages it sits beside Play lineup, or beside "No shows yet". On band and
  show pages it sits on the corner of the band's picture, like on Bands, so it never takes a line of its own.
- In the car, the same look on full and half screen. On full screen the voice hint and Play lineup share a row.
- Venue photos are kept sharper (up to 1600 pixels across) so they fill the header on any screen. The venue and band
  forms now suggest the best photo: wide for venues, square for bands.
- The logo is just TOPA over LOCAL MUSIC, without the ridge above it, so it's lighter in the top bar and sidebar. The
  ridge stays in the opening splash. The app's name is now TOPA Local Music.
- A new app icon to match: TOPA over LOCAL MUSIC, on the home screen and in the browser tab.
- On About, Add to TOPA moved under Where shows come from, right after the ways shows get in.
- The logo moved down and opens About, so there's no separate About button. On phones TOPA sits left of Shows in the
  tab bar, and the top bar is just Ventura County and search. In the car the logo sits at the bottom of the sidebar,
  where About was.

## 2.5.1

**Fixes.**

- A show listed without a venue no longer appears as a blank venue. It shows as "Venue not listed" with its town, and
  links to its listing.
- Instagram shows as an @handle link instead of a big round button: on venue pages under the address, on band
  pages under the name, and for both the band and the venue on show pages. A band's other links sit on the same line.
- Show pages no longer repeat the band's name above its links. On show and band pages the band's @handle sits under
  its play button, and moves up when there's no preview to play.
- Show pages put the distance in the line above the band's name, like venue pages, instead of on a line of its own.
- On phones, band, venue and show pages are one color behind the logo again, with no line through the top bar.
